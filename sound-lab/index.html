How to use it. Press Play to hear the lone EDM beat, then pick up power-ups with the cards or keys 1 to 8. Every layer is playing silently in sync from the start, so a pickup only opens its fader on the next bar line. “Take control” drops the rock drum kit in on the next bar with a crash, and lets it fade at the bar line when you let go. “Take a hit” triggers Dropout: the newest layer cuts out on the next eighth note and returns on a bar line after the Dropout length.

Gear sets. Power Metal, Synthwave and Orchestral all work the same way. Each set has five armor pieces (head, back, forearms, waist, legs) and weapons that use the Rig’s hands: one two-handed weapon that counts as two pieces, or two one-handed weapons. Each piece plays its member as soon as you equip it. One to three pieces sound loose and muffled, four or more lock together, and piloting brings them to full power. Only one armor piece fits each body slot, so sets can be mixed, but only one can be complete at a time. A full set unlocks its special (X, every 30 seconds): time slows for a bar, then the music snaps back for a solo while you are invincible for 10 seconds. With the two one-handed weapons the solo becomes a duel. A full set plus Pilot mode fires the set bonus: the lead vocal comes in and everything else ducks under.

Everything you hear is synthesized live in your browser. No samples or recordings are used, so there is nothing to license. These are placeholder sounds to test the system and the arrangement, not final music.
